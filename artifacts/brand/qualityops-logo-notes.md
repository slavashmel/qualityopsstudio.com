# QualityOps Studio Logo Directions

## Recommendation

Use **02 System Mark + 01 Premium Wordmark** as the primary direction.

Why:

- It feels sober, premium, and engineering-led.
- It avoids generic QA symbols such as bugs, shields, checkmarks, magnifiers, and gears.
- The mark can scale down to favicon and LinkedIn avatar.
- The wordmark feels like a boutique advisory studio rather than a commodity QA vendor.

## Direction Notes

### 01 Premium Wordmark

Best for the website header, proposals, invoices, and decks. It feels the most expensive because it does not over-explain itself.

### 02 System Mark

Best practical identity. The connected nodes imply quality gates, ownership, dependencies, and a system that can be inspected and redesigned.

### 03 QOS Monogram

Most brandable, but less immediately clear. Could work later if the brand becomes known.

### 04 Release Signal

Strong storytelling option: chaotic signals become aligned release confidence. Good for hero graphics or proposal section dividers.

### 05 Operating Loop

Best conceptual metaphor for quality as an operating system across people, process, automation, and release governance.

### 06 Advisory Lockup

Closest to a consulting/advisory brand, but the cross-like symbol needs care so it does not feel medical or generic.

## Next Iteration
