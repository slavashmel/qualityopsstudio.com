# QualityOps Studio Cross Refinement

The original square/cross wins in miniature because it is brutally simple. The right next move is not to replace it with a complex concept, but to add one small detail that changes the reading.

## Goal

Keep:

- dark square;
- simple cross;
- strong favicon readability;
- premium advisory feel.

Change:

- reduce medical association;
- add a hint of quality systems, release signal, Q, or process path.

## Best Directions

### 03 Quality Path

My favorite. The golden diagonal turns the mark into a process path through the quality system. It is still simple, but it says more than a plain cross.

### 07 Corner Signal

Very clean. The cross stays intact, while a small corner signal/dot gives it a brand detail. This is the safest visual evolution.

### 08 Broken Cross

Most minimal and least medical. The cross becomes four system segments around a central node. It is more abstract, but very strong in small sizes.

## Lower Priority

### 04 Q Cut

More ownable because of the Q, but it becomes a different logo family.

### 05 Four Nodes

Good idea, but too busy for favicon.

### 06 Release Gate Cut

Conceptually useful, but slightly mechanical.

## Recommendation

Try **03 Quality Path** on the website first. If it feels too active, use **07 Corner Signal**. If we want the least medical and most minimal version, use **08 Broken Cross**.
