# QA Consulting System

## Core Positioning

**English:** I help scaling engineering teams turn QA from a release bottleneck into a predictable quality system: faster regression, clearer ownership, smarter automation, and calmer releases.

**Russian:** Помогаю инженерным командам превратить QA из узкого места в управляемую систему качества: быстрее регрессия, понятнее ownership, меньше ручной рутины, спокойнее релизы.

This should not be sold as generic testing, outsourced QA, or test automation contracting. The strongest angle is **QA/process transformation for teams where quality has already become an organizational constraint**.

## Ideal Buyer

- CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, Head of Product, or founder.
- B2B SaaS, fintech, gaming/iGaming, platform, telecom, infrastructure, or enterprise software teams.
- Rough company stage: 30-300 employees, 5-80 engineers, 3-20 QA/QE specialists.
- The team is shipping, but quality work has become slower, harder to trust, or harder to coordinate.
- They may not be ready to hire a full-time Head of QA, but they need senior quality leadership.

## Core Problems

- Regression takes too long and blocks frequent releases.
- Automation exists, but the team does not trust the signal.
- QA depends on hidden knowledge, manual coordination, or individual heroics.
- Jira, Confluence, and TMS are present but do not create useful visibility.
- QA engineers execute tasks but do not own product quality.
- Release readiness is subjective and stressful.
- Test data, environments, dependencies, and CI/CD ownership slow the team down.
- AI tools are appearing, but there is no practical QA workflow around them.

## Differentiation

- Technical enough to inspect CI/CD, Kubernetes, logs, test data, API/UI automation, performance testing, and internal tooling.
- Senior enough to design operating models, team ownership, hiring, onboarding, metrics, and stakeholder reporting.
- Evidence-backed impact:
  - Cross-team regression reduced from 10 hours to 30 minutes.
  - QA automation costs reduced by about 50%.
  - Distributed QA organization scaled to 30+ engineers.
  - Cross-functional onboarding reduced ramp-up by 1-2 weeks.
  - Jira dependency process and AI-assisted workflows introduced.
- Public proof can include Russian-language talks when relevant:
  - SecureOffice fast E2E testing approach.
  - Winfinity regression reduction.
  - Winfinity onboarding process.

## Offer Ladder

### 1. Quality System Diagnostic

**Format:** 2-3 weeks, fixed scope.

**Promise:** Find where the quality system is losing time, trust, and predictability.

**Activities:**

- Stakeholder interviews.
- QA/release workflow mapping.
- Jira, Confluence, and TMS review.
- Regression and automation review.
- CI/CD, environment, and test data review.
- Ownership and communication analysis.

**Deliverables:**

- Current-state map.
- Bottleneck and risk report.
- Quick wins for 2-4 weeks.
- 30/60/90-day roadmap.
- Executive workshop.

**Commercial role:** Entry product. Easier to buy than open-ended consulting.

### 2. Regression Rescue Sprint

**Format:** 4-8 weeks.

**Promise:** Reduce regression pain and make release confidence visible.

**Activities:**

- Regression suite triage.
- Test prioritization and risk-based selection.
- Flaky-test strategy.
- Parallelization and CI/CD improvements.
- Test data and environment improvements.
- Reporting and release quality gates.

**Deliverables:**

- Prioritized regression model.
- CI/CD and execution improvement plan.
- Release confidence dashboard or reporting model.
- Ownership model for maintenance.
- Handoff documentation.

**Commercial role:** Flagship implementation offer because the personal proof point is strong: 10 hours to 30 minutes.

### 3. Fractional Head of QA

**Format:** 1-3 days/week for 2-6 months.

**Promise:** Senior QA leadership without a full-time Head of QA hire.

**Activities:**

- QA strategy and operating model.
- QA lead mentoring.
- Hiring/interview support.
- Onboarding system design.
- Metrics, OKRs, review cadence.
- Automation governance.
- Cross-team dependency process.
- Stakeholder reporting.

**Deliverables:**

- QA operating model.
- Team ownership structure.
- QA roadmap.
- Hiring/interview scorecards.
- Onboarding materials.
- Review rhythm and reporting structure.

**Commercial role:** Higher-retainer offer after trust is built through the diagnostic.

## First 30-Day Plan

### Week 1: Package

- Finalize one-page diagnostic offer.
- Decide pricing hypothesis.
- Prepare 2 case-study drafts:
  - Regression 10h -> 30m.
  - Onboarding / QA ownership system.
- Polish website copy and visual direction.

### Week 2: Proof and Outreach Assets

- Write 3 LinkedIn posts:
  - Why regression gets slow.
  - Why QA ownership fails.
  - How to audit a QA system before hiring more people.
- Create a short diagnostic checklist PDF or webpage.
- Prepare outbound message variants for CTO/VP Engineering.

### Week 3: Market Test

- Reach out to 20-30 warm or semi-warm contacts.
- Ask for 5 discovery conversations, not sales.
- Test which pain gets the strongest reaction:
  - regression speed,
  - QA ownership,
  - automation trust,
  - release confidence,
  - AI-assisted QA workflows.

### Week 4: Offer Refinement

- Update website language from real conversations.
- Narrow ICP if one segment responds better.
- Turn the best conversation into a concrete paid diagnostic proposal.
- Decide whether to keep the brand personal or create a separate consulting brand.
